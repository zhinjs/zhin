Let the structured-decision provider decide group ambient engagement. New `ai.decisions.ambient` policy (`off` / `shadow` / `active`, plus `timeoutMs` and `minConfidence`): in `active` mode the DecisionProvider (for example TypeSafe Jev) answers one binary `engage` / `stay_quiet` choice over the same recent messages the chat-model judge sees, and the `ai.trigger.ambient.judge` list takes over when it fails, times out or answers invalidly; in `shadow` mode the judge list still decides and the decision is logged as `ambient_decision_shadow`. Quiet time, cooldown, hourly caps, conversation window, judge interval, `ambient.minConfidence` and ambient shadow mode are unchanged, and addressed turns still skip the ambient judge. Without `ai.decisions.ambient` nothing changes.
