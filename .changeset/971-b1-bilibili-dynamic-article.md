#971 B1: Experimental Bilibili adapter now polls and drafts replies for whitelisted 动态 and 专栏 comments, in addition to videos. New `dynamics` (dynamic ids, quoted strings) and `articles` (cv ids) whitelists; a blank list polls nothing of that kind. Each dynamic is resolved once at start through `x/polymer/web-dynamic/v1/detail`, trusting the platform-declared `basic.comment_type` / `basic.comment_id_str` (image posts → `album:<相簿id>` type 11, text/forward posts → `dynamic:<dynamic id>` type 17); video/article dynamics and unsupported or deleted ones are skipped with a log instead of guessing an oid. Articles map to `article:<cvid>` type 12. Conversation ids stay `<resource>:<oid>:<rootRpid>` with parent `<resource>:<oid>`, so the prefix alone fixes the reply type. `x/msgfeed/reply|at` notifications accept business 11/17 and 12 only when the matching whitelist is configured (still filtered by `notificationScope`); 19-digit dynamic ids lost by JSON numbers are recovered from the notification link and cross-checked, otherwise dropped. Drafts carry the comment type; `send` still only queues (`review` / `off`, no `auto`), approval posts `x/v2/reply/add` with the draft's type. Risk-code circuit breaking now also covers dynamic detail lookups.
