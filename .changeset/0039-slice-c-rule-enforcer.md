ADR 0039 slice C: approved group rules are now enforced. A new host-side `GroupRuleEnforcer` (reached through `AgentTurnIngressRoute.enforceGroupRule`) takes a reported violation and runs the current stage of the group's active rule for that trigger: `remind` / `warn` send a template reminder that @-mentions the member (control plane + session profile gated, optional recall of the original message); `mute` runs automatically with **no approval card**, using the rule's approval as the hard-gate approval (`ToolHardGate.checkPreApproved` now accepts `source: 'rule'`, reports `rule_hash_mismatch` for a tampered rule, and passes `ops_cooldown` when the gate has one), still stopped by `emergency_stop` / `read_only`; `suggest_kick` never kicks by itself — it raises a fresh `member` hard-gate card (deduplicated by `ops_cooldown`) and kicks only after a human approves. Owners/admins and whitelisted members are skipped (`rule_hit_exempt`); when the bot cannot moderate, it records `rule_hit_bot_not_admin` and tells the group once a day. Every hit writes one `kind=rule_enforcement` audit row that shares its `turn_id` with the hard-gate and outbound rows. Ladder progress is in memory only, capped and pruned after the quiet window; nothing new is written to disk. No detector calls the enforcer yet: `flood` (slice D) and the content-moderation migration (slice E) come next.
